Welcome to Pelican Town

Step into Stardew Valley, a relaxing farming life sim where you leave the city behind to restore your grandfather's farm. Explore the valley, grow your crops, raise animals, fish, mine, and forge friendships with the townspeople.

Gameplay Features

  • Choose one out of eight farm layouts to build your farm.

  • Farm, fish, mine, and raise livestock.

  • Build relationships with the villagers, and find someone special.

  • Customize your character with unique outfits, accessories, and more.

  • Cook meals and craft useful items.

  • Battle monsters and enemies while exploring the mines.
